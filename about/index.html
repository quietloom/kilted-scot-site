The studio credit, explained

About kilted.scot

A small Scottish documentary studio — one channel, one standard of evidence, a separate series for each thread worth pulling.

What "a kilted.scot production" means

Every script, every source, and every editorial call — what makes the cut, what's labelled legend rather than fact — is written and decided by a human. The narration you hear is a synthetic voice, and any illustration standing in for a photograph that doesn't exist is AI-generated. Both are disclosed on every video, per YouTube's altered-content policy — not buried in a description nobody reads.

Why kilted.scot and not a channel name

kilted.scot is the studio, not a series. Unsolved Scotland is the first thing it's made; when a second series exists, it gets its own name and its own identity too — kilted.scot is what ties them together, not what they're branded as.
